What the classification means

Cases use the category reported in the booking record reviewed by the referring partner: conviction reported, pending charge reported, immigration-only, or not reported. A pending charge is not a conviction.

Confidentiality

Names, A-Numbers, birth dates, contact details, and case documents are not published. Contributions go to the organization named on each case, not directly to the person in detention.
