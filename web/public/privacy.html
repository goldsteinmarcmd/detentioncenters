Privacy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Public detention data

The map publishes facility-level aggregates. It does not publish detainee names, A-Numbers, birth dates, contact information, or confidential bond records.

Optional analytics

Analytics run only after you select Accept. We then count page views, clicks, sessions, approximate device type, country derived from an IP address in memory, referrer, and campaign parameters. Raw IP addresses, search text, precise location, and detainee information are not stored.

A random browser identifier is stored locally to calculate unique and total use. Declining sends no analytics and stores only your choice.

Affiliate lodging links

Some lodging links may contain affiliate attribution. If a qualifying reservation is completed, the provider may pay a commission intended for the bond release fund at no additional cost to the traveler. The lodging provider handles the reservation and its own tracking.
